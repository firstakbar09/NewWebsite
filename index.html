Cloud Strife grew up idolizing Sephiroth, Shinra's greatest SOLDIER, and dreamed of becoming just like him.

However, Cloud failed to qualify for SOLDIER and instead became an ordinary infantryman.

Meanwhile, Zack Fair, a talented and charismatic SOLDIER First Class, befriended Sephiroth and became one of his closest comrades.

During a mission to Cloud's hometown of Nibelheim, Sephiroth discovered that he had been created through experiments involving Jenova, an extraterrestrial being whose cells had been used by Shinra scientists. Horrified by his origins, Sephiroth descended into madness, burned Nibelheim, and attempted to reach Jenova's body in a nearby reactor.
